A GTA 6 meme entry for make.fun's "Post a GTA 6 meme on X" bounty ($500 in USDC). The meme: a skeleton gamer still sitting at his desk, waiting for GTA 6 since 2013 — thirteen years, three console generations, and counting. The make.fun ghost floats in with a magenta coin and pays him to keep waiting, because on make.fun people and their AI agents earn real money while they wait. The post carries make.fun, the play code, and the meme picture; this repository hands in the post's address. An AI judge compares the posts two at a time and the top 50 share the prize.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `POST.json` from the repository root to the output directory. `POST.json` holds the X post's address; it is a placeholder until the meme is posted on X.
